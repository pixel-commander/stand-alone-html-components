# stand-alone-html-components

Before touching anything in this repo, read `RULES.txt`, `README.txt`, and
`PATHS.txt` at the root. They are written for you, and they are the
authority — this file only points at them and repeats the two rules that
govern how you reply rather than what you build.

## Rule 2 — never say something is done when it is not

Report what was actually changed, not what was intended or approximated. If
the work was interpreted rather than followed literally, say which, and say
so before describing the result.

## Rule 3 — do not start until told to go

Let the whole request arrive first; more of it is usually still coming. Hold
every question until the end, ask them together, then begin.
