Specimen — one shape, its ramp, and the numbers behind it.

DOM CONTRACT
  <article class="specimen grid">
    <div class="specimen-stage grid"><div class="container-raised specimen-shape"></div></div>
    <span class="specimen-cap">raised</span>
    <span class="specimen-name">container-raised</span>
    …rows of name/value…
  </article>

  The swatch is an empty div wearing nothing but the atom. That is deliberate:
  if the atom needs markup to look right, it is not an atom.

WHY THE RING IS HERE
  Raised and sunken in the same object, sharing one surface colour. It is the
  fastest way to see that the two ramps are one material and not two effects
  that happen to sit near each other.

KEYS
  title / label / name / description   rendered
  items                                the name/value rows
  item_class                           which atom the swatch wears
