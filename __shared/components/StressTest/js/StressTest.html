The same atom on three backgrounds. It survives all three because every layer is an alpha over the two theme colours, so it multiplies into whatever it lands on. An opaque two-colour shadow — the shorter spec most generators emit — paints over the background instead, and becomes a visible grey halo the moment it leaves the one surface it was sampled from. That is the whole reason this system is written in alpha and why it can be handed to a team.
